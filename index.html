About the Studio

A Space for Creative Souls

Sunday Living is more than an art studio, it is a sanctuary for those who seek to express themselves through color, texture, and form. Founded by @nims.okplease, we believe art is not just about creating, it is about living.

Nestled above Personality Ikon salon in Model Town, our studio offers a warm, inviting atmosphere where beginners and seasoned artists alike can explore painting, sketching, mixed media, and therapeutic art sessions.
